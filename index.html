How to play
  1. Students join on their own devices with the code above and pick their team. The team whose turn it is can tap Roll (you can roll too).
  2. Landing on a space opens the next question from that part of the board. The team types answers on their devices (or in Zoom chat). They pop up on the card, then press Show the answer.
  3. Checkpoints stop the first team to reach each new part. That question goes to the whole class, and any team with a strong answer can earn stars.
  4. Award 1–3 stars for the best answer. Part 6 questions are bonus spaces worth double (2, 4 or 6 stars).
  5. Plot Twists shake up the board. Finishing earns +5, +3, then +1. Most stars wins.
60-minute run sheet
  • 0–5 Welcome and split into 6 teams
  • 5–12 Part 1: Hooks & Beginnings (Q1–4)
  • 12–20 Part 2: Dialogue & Character (Q5–8)
  • 20–32 Part 3: Show, Don't Tell (Q9–14)
  • 32–40 Part 4: Active Verbs (Q15–18)
  • 40–51 Part 5: Pacing (Q19–24)
  • 51–60 Part 6: Writer's Challenge (Q25–27) and final scores

With 6 teams, keep each turn to about a minute (use the 30s timer). If a part's questions run out, the card pulls an unused question from another part. Running long? Press End game to show final scores.
